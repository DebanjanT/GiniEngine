Terrain Textures
================

Place your terrain textures here. Supported formats: PNG, JPG, TGA

Recommended texture names (will be auto-loaded):
- Grass.png
- Dirt.png  
- Rock.png
- Road.png

Texture requirements:
- Power of 2 dimensions recommended (256x256, 512x512, 1024x1024)
- Seamless/tileable textures work best
- RGB or RGBA format

You can download free terrain textures from:
- https://ambientcg.com/
- https://polyhaven.com/textures
- https://textures.com/
